UPI Guard

A second pair of eyes on every UPI payment.

UPI Guard checks who you are paying, how the request reached you and whether it looks like a known scam, before any money leaves your account.

A trained model and readable rules score every payment in real time.
High-risk payments are held for a human analyst instead of being sent.
Analyst decisions feed back into the model, so new scams are caught faster.
Sandbox build: balances are simulated, no real money moves.
